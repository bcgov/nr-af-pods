Tree Fruit Climate Resiliency Program - Claim for Reimbursement

The goal of the Tree Fruit Climate Resiliency Program is to increase the resilience of B.C.’s tree fruit sector by providing cost-share reimbursements to eligible farm businesses to address risks related to extreme weather events that are impacting existing orchards and operations.

This program is administered on behalf of the British Columbia Ministry of Agriculture and Food by MNP LLP (‘Program Management’). You can expect to receive a response from Program Management through the programmanagement@mnp.ca​ email in the next few days following your claim submission. You are also welcome to direct any additional questions to this email.

Instructions

  • Complete all sections of the claim for payment form.
  • Attach copies of invoices and corresponding proofs of payment.
